Why Your Website May Be Losing Customers

You can spend thousands on advertising and SEO, but if your website has usability friction, poor mobile layout, slow loading speeds, or confusing call-to-actions, prospective clients will leave within seconds for your competitors.

My website audit doesn't just run automated scripts. As an experienced full-stack developer and designer, I manually inspect your website page by page to pinpoint technical bugs, visual inconsistencies, and psychological barriers preventing conversions.

What Every Audit Examines

UI & Visual Hierarchy

Clarity of typography, color contrast, brand consistency, whitespace, and visual focal points that guide user attention.

Mobile Responsiveness

Testing on iOS, Android, and tablets to catch overflow glitches, unclickable elements, and layout collapses.

Performance & Web Vitals

Deep-dive into LCP (Largest Contentful Paint), INP, CLS layout shifts, heavy assets, and server response times.

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

Friction in checkout flows, lead form drop-offs, trust badges, call-to-action placement, and copy readability.

On-Page Technical SEO

Heading hierarchies (H1–H3), meta titles/descriptions, broken links, canonicals, robots.txt, and schema tags.

Security & Console Errors

HTTPS configuration, mixed content warnings, JavaScript console errors, and outdated plugin vulnerabilities.

What You Receive in the Audit Deliverable

  • Annotated Screenshot PDF: Visual breakdown of exact issues with side-by-side solutions.
  • Prioritized Action Plan: Issues ranked by Quick Wins (high impact, low effort), Medium fixes, and Strategic overhauls.
  • Developer-Ready Technical Notes: Exact CSS selectors, code snippets, and plugin recommendations your developer (or I) can implement right away.
  • 30-Minute Video / Call Walkthrough: Clear, jargon-free explanation of the findings and answers to all your questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a website UX/UI audit report?
The audit includes a thorough evaluation of user experience (UX), visual design hierarchy (UI), mobile responsiveness across devices, PageSpeed & Core Web Vitals, conversion funnel obstacles, on-page SEO issues, and broken elements. You receive an annotated PDF report with high-priority fixes categorized by effort and business impact.
How long does a website audit take?
A preliminary quick audit summary can be provided within 24 hours. A complete, in-depth technical and UX audit report is typically delivered in 2 to 3 business days.
Can you fix the issues discovered in the audit?
Yes! Because I am a full-stack developer with 8+ years experience, I can directly implement all the recommended fixes — whether it requires CSS adjustments, PHP/Laravel refactoring, WordPress speed tuning, or Shopify CRO enhancements.
What do I need to share to get started?
Simply share your website URL, your primary business goals (e.g., more leads, higher online store checkouts), and any known pain points. No backend access is required for an initial audit.
