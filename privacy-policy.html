Privacy Policy

Your trust, personal data, and business intellectual property are deeply respected. Here is how your information is safeguarded.

Last Updated: September 2026 Data Protection Standards SSL Secured

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Shashi Kumar ("we", "our", "us", operated by Shashi) collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when you visit https://skdigital.cloud or communicate with us through our inquiry forms, email, or WhatsApp.

By accessing this website or submitting an inquiry, you acknowledge the terms outlined in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to communicate with you and deliver professional web development, optimization, bug fixing, and software testing services:

  • Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and business name provided voluntarily in our inquiry forms.
  • Project Details: Information regarding your website URL, technology stack, project scope, budget preferences, and design specifications.
  • Technical Access Credentials (Clients only): When under contract for web development, bug fixing, or server maintenance, you may securely provide FTP/SFTP, cPanel, database, or Git access. These credentials are used solely to perform contracted work and are deleted upon project completion.
  • Standard Web Analytics: Non-personally identifiable metrics (such as browser type, operating system, and pages visited) to evaluate website performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is used strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To prepare customized quotes, technical assessments, and proposals.
  • To communicate project milestones, deliverables, and technical updates.
  • To issue invoices, receipts, and process milestone payments.
  • To provide ongoing website maintenance and priority technical support.
We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or contact details to third parties, data aggregators, or marketing lists. Period.

4. Client Confidentiality & Code Ownership

We treat all client business concepts, algorithms, proprietary workflows, and user databases as strictly confidential:

  • Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs): We are glad to sign formal mutual NDAs prior to reviewing proprietary source code or confidential business requirements.
  • Intellectual Property: Unless agreed otherwise in writing, upon final milestone payment, you own 100% of all custom code, stylesheets, graphic assets, and databases developed specifically for your project.

5. WhatsApp, Google Analytics & Third-Party Services

When you click WhatsApp chat links on this website, your interaction is handled by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.) under their own privacy policy and end-to-end encryption standards. We also use secure email servers to respond to inquiries. Additionally, we use Google Analytics (Google LLC) to collect aggregated, anonymized usage insights to help us analyze visitor trends and optimize website performance.

6. Data Security Measures

We employ industry-standard technical measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your information:

  • Full site-wide HTTPS encryption with modern TLS certificates.
  • Encrypted credential storage (e.g. password managers) for any server keys shared by clients.
  • Routine purging of staging environment credentials once deployment is signed off.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right at any time to:

  • Request a copy of any personal contact data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or updating of your contact information.
  • Request complete deletion of your inquiry details from our communication logs.

To exercise any of these rights, please send an email to shashi.php92@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Request".
