Frequently Asked Cost & Project Questions

How accurate is this cost calculator estimate?

The calculator delivers an accurate baseline range reflecting standard engineering hours, technology stacks, and page structures. When you click "Get My Quote on WhatsApp", I review your specific requirements, wireframes, or reference links to deliver a finalized fixed quotation.

What payment terms do you accept?

Work is invoiced on clear milestones: typically 30% advance on kickoff, 40% upon staging milestone approval, and 30% final balance upon production launch and complete handover. International payments are accepted via Wire Transfer, Wise, PayPal, or Stripe. Domestic Indian payments via UPI, NEFT, or IMPS.

What if my project requires features not listed in the calculator?

No problem at all. Yes. Every project includes a 14 to 30-day post-launch warranty for bug fixes. Afterward, you can choose an ongoing Monthly Maintenance & Security AMC starting from ₹2,999 / $45 per month covering automated backups, uptime monitoring, core updates, and priority developer hours on call.
