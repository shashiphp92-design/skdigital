Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about working together.

How quickly can we start my project?
For urgent bug fixing or performance audits, I can typically begin within 24 to 48 hours. For full website builds or custom web applications, we will first confirm requirements and start as soon as initial milestones are agreed upon.
Do you sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)?
Absolutely. Client confidentiality and IP protection are paramount. I am happy to sign your NDA before reviewing sensitive business ideas, databases, or proprietary code.
How are payments structured?
For fixed-scope projects, payments are typically split into milestones (e.g., 30% upfront deposit, 40% midway upon demo review, and 30% upon final delivery/deployment). For ongoing maintenance, monthly invoicing is used. I accept Bank Wire, UPI (India), PayPal, and international transfers (Wise / Stripe).
Can you work with my existing developer team or agency?
Yes. I frequently collaborate with in-house design teams, external marketing agencies, and other engineers using Git, Jira/Trello, Slack, and standard pull-request workflows.
