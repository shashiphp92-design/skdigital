Frequently Asked Questions

Which platform is best for my website: WordPress, Laravel or React?
WordPress is ideal for business and content websites that need easy editing. Shopify or WooCommerce suits online stores. Laravel, Node.js and React are best for custom web applications with complex logic, dashboards, user accounts and integrations. I recommend the right stack after understanding your goals.
Will my website be mobile friendly and SEO optimized?
Yes. Every website is built mobile-first, responsive on all devices, and includes on-page SEO: semantic HTML, meta tags, schema markup, XML sitemap, fast loading speed and Google Search Console setup.
Do you provide hosting and domain setup?
Yes. I help you choose and set up hosting (cPanel, VPS, AWS, DigitalOcean), configure your domain, SSL certificate and email, and deploy the website.
