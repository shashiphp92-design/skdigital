Why Independent Testing Matters

Developers test what they built to work. A dedicated QA tester tests how real users will actually use — and break — your product. Bugs found after launch cost 10–100x more to fix than bugs found before. As both a developer and tester, I understand where bugs hide and how to reproduce them clearly so they get fixed fast.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I receive after testing?
A detailed bug report with severity, steps to reproduce, screenshots/screen recordings, device/browser details and suggested fixes — delivered in a shared sheet, Jira, Trello or your preferred tool. I can also fix the bugs I find.
Do you do automation testing?
Yes. I write automated test suites with Selenium, Playwright, Cypress, PHPUnit, Jest and Postman/Newman for API tests, and integrate them into CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI).
Do you test on real mobile devices?
Yes. I test on real Android and iOS devices across multiple screen sizes and OS versions, plus emulators and remote device testing labs (BrowserStack) for wider coverage.
