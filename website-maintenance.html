Your Website Needs Ongoing Care

A website is not a one-time project. Software updates, security threats, browser changes, plugin conflicts, expiring SSL certificates and content changes all need attention. Neglected websites get hacked, slow down, break after updates and lose Google rankings. A maintenance plan is cheaper than an emergency fix — and far cheaper than lost customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need website maintenance?
Websites need regular updates to stay secure, fast and compatible. Without maintenance, outdated plugins and software become security risks, performance degrades, and small issues turn into costly downtime. A maintenance plan prevents problems before they affect your business.
Can I cancel the maintenance plan anytime?
Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no long-term lock-in. Annual plans are available with a discount if you prefer.
Do you maintain websites you didn't build?
Absolutely. I start with a full audit of your existing website, fix any pressing issues, and then take over ongoing maintenance regardless of who built it.
